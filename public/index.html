How to play

Krillion gives you a question and wants the rarest answer. KrONE turns it around.

  1. Every day you get 7 rare answers.
  2. For each one, write the rarest question you can think of that still has that answer. You have 60 seconds.
  3. The question has to be true and has to lead to the answer, and it can't contain the answer.
  4. The rarer your angle, the higher you climb. Questions everybody asks barely leave the ground.

Rarity comes from an AI judge, how far your question is from the obvious ones, and how many other players asked something similar today. One of ONE means nobody else came close. Each point is 10 m, so a perfect day reaches 7,000 m.
